MACHINE LEARNING ENGINEER
Turn machine learning models into reliable, measurable and scalable production capabilities.

EMPLOYMENT
Full-time • Permanent • Hybrid or on-site depending on business needs
LOCATION

THE OPPORTUNITY
We are seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to design, build and operate production-grade machine learning solutions. The role bridges data science and software engineering, with responsibility for model development, deployment, evaluation, monitoring and continuous improvement.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Translate business problems into measurable machine learning objectives and technical requirements.
- Develop, train, evaluate and optimise machine learning models and supporting data pipelines.
- Build maintainable services, APIs and batch or streaming inference workflows.
- Establish reproducible experimentation, versioning, testing and model release processes.
- Deploy models to cloud, on-premises or hybrid environments using appropriate platform patterns.
- Monitor model quality, drift, latency, cost and reliability, and implement feedback loops.
- Apply responsible AI, privacy, security, explainability and governance requirements throughout the lifecycle.
- Collaborate with data scientists, software engineers, platform teams, domain experts and product stakeholders.
- Document architecture, assumptions, datasets, evaluations, operational procedures and limitations.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Data Science or a related quantitative field, or comparable practical experience.
- Strong Python skills and experience with common machine learning and data-processing libraries.
- Solid understanding of supervised and unsupervised learning, evaluation methods and statistical reasoning.
- Experience building tested software components, APIs and data pipelines.
- Familiarity with containers, source control, CI/CD and production monitoring.
- Ability to explain model behaviour, trade-offs and limitations to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Strong problem-solving, experimentation and teamwork skills.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience with MLOps platforms, feature stores, model registries or distributed processing.
- Knowledge of deep learning, NLP, computer vision or generative AI.
- Experience deploying models in cloud, edge, on-premises or regulated environments.
- Familiarity with responsible AI, data governance and model risk management.

TYPICAL TECHNOLOGY LANDSCAPE
Python • scikit-learn / PyTorch / TensorFlow • SQL • APIs • Containers • CI/CD • Model Registry • Monitoring

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
- Models deliver measurable value against agreed business and technical metrics.
- Training and deployment workflows are reproducible, tested and automated.
- Production behaviour is observable, with drift and quality issues detected early.
- Solutions remain maintainable, secure and appropriately governed throughout their lifecycle.
