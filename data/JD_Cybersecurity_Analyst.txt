CYBERSECURITY ANALYST
Detect, investigate and reduce cyber risk through disciplined security operations and continuous improvement.

EMPLOYMENT
Full-time • Permanent • Hybrid or on-site depending on business needs
LOCATION
Flexible by role and project requirements

THE OPPORTUNITY
We are looking for a Cybersecurity Analyst to help protect the organisation’s systems, data and users. The role will monitor security signals, investigate suspicious activity, support incident response and strengthen controls in collaboration with technology and business teams.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Monitor and triage security alerts from endpoint, identity, email, network, cloud and application sources.
- Investigate suspicious activity, determine scope and impact, and document evidence and conclusions.
- Support containment, eradication, recovery and post-incident review activities.
- Maintain and improve detection rules, playbooks, dashboards and case-management workflows.
- Conduct vulnerability reviews, track remediation and validate risk-reduction actions.
- Contribute to identity, endpoint, cloud, data protection and security awareness initiatives.
- Produce clear operational metrics, incident reports and risk-based recommendations.
- Support audits, control assessments, policy implementation and security exception processes.
- Collaborate with IT operations, engineering, legal, privacy and business stakeholders.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science or an equivalent field, or comparable practical experience.
- Understanding of common attack techniques, security controls, incident response and vulnerability management.
- Experience with security monitoring, investigation or administration tools.
- Knowledge of endpoint, identity, network, cloud and email security fundamentals.
- Ability to analyse logs, correlate events and communicate technical findings clearly.
- Strong judgement, attention to detail, integrity and respect for confidentiality.
- Ability to prioritise work based on business impact and risk.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience with SIEM, EDR, SOAR, threat intelligence or cloud security platforms.
- Familiarity with recognised security frameworks and control standards.
- Basic scripting or data-analysis skills for investigation and automation.
- Relevant entry-level or professional cybersecurity certification.

TYPICAL TECHNOLOGY LANDSCAPE
SIEM • EDR • Identity Security • Cloud Security • Vulnerability Management • Threat Intelligence • SOAR • Python / KQL

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
- Security alerts are assessed consistently and material incidents are escalated promptly.
- Investigations produce clear evidence, decisions and remediation actions.
- Detection coverage and operational playbooks improve over time.
- Stakeholders receive practical, risk-based guidance that strengthens security posture.
